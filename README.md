## Before production
1. Replace placeholder SVG product/project images with images you own or are licensed to use.
2. Confirm the exact legal business name and whether you are authorized to use MRM trademarks. Keep the Mabati Dealer notice unless you have written authorization.
3. Replace relative canonical URLs and sitemap locations with your final public HTTPS domain for strongest SEO.
4. Add your verified Google Maps location.
5. Replace sample testimonials with genuine customer reviews.
6. Reconfirm all prices and accessory estimates before publishing.
