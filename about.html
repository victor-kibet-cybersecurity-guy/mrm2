More About Us

MRM, Your Building Solutions Partner

Mabati Rolling Mills Limited (MRM) is Kenya’s leading manufacturer of steel roofing and building solutions. Founded in 1962 in Mariakani, near Mombasa, MRM is the largest and flagship company of the Safal Group, which operates across multiple African nations.

MRM pioneered coated steel production in Africa using patented Aluminium-Zinc alloy technology. Licensed to the Safal Group by BIEC International and branded ZincAl, the technology is recognized for delivering superior service life to steel.

MRM operates an integrated coated-steel facility at Mariakani, producing coated and painted coated-steel products used to manufacture a wide range of building solutions across Kenya.

Key Products and Services

  • Roofing products and tiles: Residential and commercial profiles including Versatile, Elegantile, Orientile and Dumu Zas.
  • Structural solutions: Pre-engineered steel buildings, structural steel products and lightweight steel trusses.
  • Building accessories: Flashings, closures, ventilation systems and specialized construction materials.

Nationwide Footprint

MRM distributes its products through service centres, digital channels and an extensive dealer network. Its service-centre footprint includes Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Thika, Nakuru, Eldoret, Kitengela, Meru, Nyeri and Kisii.
